FAQ

Questions owners ask

Which one do I need?

If your app is live and earning but you can't read the code behind it, the Code Health Report. If your site is online but broken, Fix My Site. If it works in preview but has never been online, Go Live. Not sure? Start with the free scan.

What's in a Code Health Report?

A plain-English map of what's running inside your app, what it costs you, what will break as you grow and which security gaps to close first. Every finding points to a specific file, so you can hand it to any contractor and get an honest quote.

Do you need access to my code?

For a Code Health Report, read-only access to your repository. We never change anything. For Fix My Site and Go Live, we work in your repository and every fix is yours to keep.

Is the free scan really free?

Yes. I check your live site against all seven and email you what I find. No call, no card and no obligation to buy anything. I do these by hand, so I can only take 5 a week.

Which builders do you work with?

Apps and sites built with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor, Replit and similar AI tools. If you're not sure, send it over and we'll tell you.

How long does it take?

Usually a few working days, depending on the job and what we find. You'll get a confirmed timeline before you pay, and we keep you updated until it's done.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you get your money back. Fix My Site, Go Live and Monthly Care all come with a money-back guarantee: if we don't deliver what we promised, you get a full refund.

What happens afterwards?

You keep everything: the fixed code, the certificate or the report. If you'd like us to keep an eye on it, Monthly Care is available at £199 a month once we've done a job for you.
